FAQ

What sizes do the chamoy candies come in?

Chamoy candies come in 2 oz for $2 and 4 oz for $4.

How do I place an order?

Choose what you want from the Shop, add it to your cart, and continue to your order details.

Is pickup available?

Yes. Orders are pickup only. Pickup details are confirmed after ordering.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, Cash App, Venmo, and Zelle.

What comes in the Maruchan Kit?

The $5 kit includes Maruchan, Gansito, a choice of 2 oz Popular Chamoy Mix or spicy chips, and a choice of water or Capri Sun.

Can I order more than one item?

Yes. You can add different products and quantities to your cart before ordering.

Can I leave special instructions?

Yes. You can add special instructions when filling out your order details.
