FAQ

01What sizes do the chamoy candies come in?

Chamoy candies come in 2 oz for $2 and 4 oz for $4.

02How do I place an order?

You can add items to a cart and review an order summary. Online ordering is not available yet.

03Is pickup available?

Pickup only. The site does not send or confirm orders yet; pickup details will be available when ordering opens.

04What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, Cash App, Venmo, and Zelle.

05What comes in the Maruchan Kit?

The $5 kit includes Maruchan, Gansito, a choice of 2 oz Popular Chamoy Mix or spicy chips, and a choice of water or Capri Sun.

06Can I order more than one item?

Yes. You can add different products and quantities to your cart before ordering.

07Can I leave special instructions?

Yes. You can add special instructions when filling out your order details.
